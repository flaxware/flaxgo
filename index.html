FlaxGo is a Flaxware search portal. Your query is passed to the search provider you choose; each provider has its own privacy policy and terms. Choose the service that fits your needs.

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Minimize unnecessary personal information.
  • Check the destination before submitting a search.
  • Use privacy tools with informed expectations.
